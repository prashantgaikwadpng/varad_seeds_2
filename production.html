Seed Production at Varad Seeds

Once the products are identified in our research & development program, we work with farmers and organizers to manage the production of the respective vegetable seeds.

Currently, we are engaged in production activities in Maharashtra & Karnataka. We have technical teams in Jalna (Maharashtra) and Ranebennur (Karnataka) to monitor our production activities on a daily basis.

Our seed production features include modern methods such as shade net houses, polyhouses, and Substrates equipment (soil-less diseases systems). All seed production methods are available for farmers in India and internationally.

Experience the best in vegetable seeds production with our rigorous quality control measures, ensuring high germination rates and pure seeds. Our vegetable seeds are bred for excellence, with a focus on disease resistance and true-to-type characteristics.

Trust our high-quality vegetable seeds to deliver exceptional yields and flavor, every time. With our advanced seed technology and quality assurance processes, you can rely on our vegetable seeds to perform consistently.

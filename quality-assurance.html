Our Quality Standards

Every seed goes through a strict quality control process

Germination Testing

Every batch tested for high germination rates before reaching the farmer.

Purity Standards

Seeds are tested for genetic and physical purity to ensure true-to-type results.

Vigor Testing

Seed vigor is measured to ensure strong, uniform seedling emergence.
